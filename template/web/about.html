About Us

About Us

Community, it's what we are all about.

The Palpa District Cycling Association is dedicated to promoting cycling as a sport and a means of transportation in the Palpa district. Our mission is to foster a vibrant cycling community, encourage healthy lifestyles, and support local tourism through cycling events and activities.

  • Promote cycling as a sustainable and healthy mode of transportation.
  • Organize cycling events and competitions to engage the community.
  • Support local tourism by highlighting scenic and historical routes.
  • Advocate for cyclist-friendly infrastructure and policies.

Palpa Cycling Association

With a mission to improve lives through bicycling we teach the joys of bicycling - advocate for safe places to ride.
