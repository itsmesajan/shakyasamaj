About Us

Namaste From Bhojpur Taksar Shakya Samaj

Welcome to the website of Bhojpur Taksar Shakya Samaj. We are blessed to have a community of monks, nuns, novices, and supporters who have dedicated their lives to the practice and teachings of Buddhism. Throughout the year, we host a variety of events, retreats, and workshops designed to deepen your understanding of Buddhism and mindfulness. Central to our practice is Buddhist meditation. We believe that the stillness of the mind is the gateway to understanding oneself and the universe. Whether you are a seasoned meditator or just beginning your journey, we offer instruction and space for your practice. Check our calendar for upcoming opportunities to join our community. Join our community and start a journey of self-discovery and enrichment. May your path lead you to a profound sense of peace, happiness, and Nibbana.
